## Best at
- Coastal: credibility with named GCs and safety program.
- FLM: breadth of service/location pages, blog, pricing ranges, review widget.
- Bishop's: process transparency (live photo/video updates), family story.

## Worst at
- Coastal: no service/location pages, stale 2008/2015 tech, no residential content.
- FLM: templated location pages, duplicated form/widget, keyword-stuffed city lists, typos, no portfolio, no license.
- Bishop's: no service depth, no locations, stray KML naming another company, blank alt.
- Ron Kendall: robots.txt conflict (second `User-agent: *` group disallows all) and empty response; re-audit from a US browser.

## Opportunities
1. Full residential + commercial depth with visible license, insurance, warranty.
2. Real project case studies (scope, location, cost range, timeline).
3. Unique hyper-local copy per city, only for cities truly served.
4. Short form with photo upload, response-time promise, click/text-to-call.
5. Clean technical SEO: schema, real lastmod sitemap, alt text, fast pages.
6. Published price ranges with caveats.
